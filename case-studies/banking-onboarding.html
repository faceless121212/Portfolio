Case study

Fixing mobile banking onboarding step by step: +5% registrations

  • CompanyBank Vlasnyi Rahunok · VST Bank
  • RoleProduct Manager
  • Timeline2024 – 2025
  • LocationKyiv

TL;DR

Problem
Mobile banking onboarding is long and regulated (financial monitoring, KYC, document signing), and every step loses applicants. Document signing lost the most.
My role
Product Manager owning onboarding and KYC at VST Bank, then mobile onboarding at BVR. I also owned VST Bank's acquiring product.
Team
A product team at each bank (engineering and design), working closely with compliance and legal.
Outcome
BVR: +1–3% at each of three steps, adding up to +5% successful registrations. VST Bank: +5% onboarding conversion, and acquiring approval rate 91% → 95%.

Context and why it mattered

For a digital bank, onboarding is the acquisition funnel. Regulation fixes many of the steps, so the job is to make each mandatory step easier to finish, not to remove it.

Discovery and data

I split onboarding into individual steps and measured conversion at each one. The biggest drop-off was document signing: a mix of confusing instructions and technical failures. Financial monitoring (long questions about income and source of funds) and activation after approval were the next leaks.

Hypotheses and prioritization

Hypothesis: people accept mandatory steps when the step is short, clearly explained and technically reliable.

What I said no to:

  • Skipping KYC steps. Regulation is fixed, so we made the steps easier instead, with compliance involved from the start.
  • Many changes at once. One change per step, so each effect could be measured.

Experiments and execution

BVR

  • Financial monitoring: fewer and simpler questions, a short note explaining it's a legal requirement, and a progress indicator.
  • Document signing: clearer step-by-step instructions, fixes for technical failures, and an alternative signing method.
  • Activation: reminders for users who hadn't activated, an immediate digital card, and an incentive for the first action.

VST Bank

  • Onboarding and KYC: shorter registration, A/B tests of copy and UI, and heavy KYC steps moved later in the flow.
  • Acquiring: analysed declines by reason code and tuned antifraud rules that were declining good payments for a major retail client.

Results

MetricResult
BVR: financial monitoring, document signing, activation+1–3% each
BVR: successful registrations+5%
VST Bank: onboarding conversion+5%
VST Bank: acquiring approval rate91% → 95%

What I learned

Small gains compound. No single change was dramatic, but +1–3% at three steps added up to +5% more registrations.
