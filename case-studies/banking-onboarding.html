  • CompanyBank Vlasnyi Rahunok · VST Bank
  • RoleProduct Manager

  • LocationKyiv

TL;DR

Problem
Mobile banking onboarding is long and regulated (financial monitoring, KYC, document signing), and every step loses applicants.
My role
Product Manager owning onboarding and KYC at VST Bank, then mobile onboarding optimization at BVR. Also owned VST Bank's acquiring product.

[Add: team size and composition]
Outcome
BVR: +1–3% at each of the financial monitoring, document signing and activation steps, adding up to +5% successful registrations. VST Bank: +5% onboarding conversion and acquiring approval rate 91% → 95%.

Context and why it mattered

For a digital bank, onboarding is the acquisition funnel. Regulation fixes many of the steps, so the work is about making each mandatory step easier to finish rather than removing it.
