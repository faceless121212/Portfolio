Case study

BVR Pay: 93% payment conversion at 10k+ users

  • CompanyBank Vlasnyi Rahunok (Fozzy Group)
  • RoleProduct Owner, BVR Pay
  • TimelineJul 2024 – Oct 2025
  • LocationKyiv

TL;DR

Problem
The bank's own payment method had to work inside Silpo's e-commerce checkout, where every failed payment is a lost order.
My role
Product Owner of BVR Pay. Worked with the CPO on digital payments, loyalty integration and new product experiments.
Team
Cross-company: the bank's product team, Silpo's e-commerce team and the CPO.
Outcome
BVR Pay live in Silpo e-commerce for 10,000+ users. Successful payment conversion went from 88% to 93% (+5 points).

Context and why it mattered

Bank Vlasnyi Rahunok and the Silpo retail chain are both part of Fozzy Group. A native payment method in Silpo's online store served three goals at once:

  • Grow bank customers by reaching Silpo's shoppers.
  • Ecosystem loyalty: tie payments to Silpo's loyalty programme.
  • Payment revenue: keep transaction revenue inside the group instead of paying external acquirers.

Discovery and data

I broke failed payments down by reason code instead of looking at one overall success rate. Failures fell into three groups:

  • Bank declines: insufficient funds, limits and antifraud rules.
  • Technical errors: timeouts and integration failures between Silpo and the bank.
  • Checkout UX: confusing choices and extra steps.

Hypotheses and prioritization

Hypothesis: most technical failures and some declines can be recovered if the integration is more reliable and users get a clear error with an easy retry.

What I said no to:

  • New payment methods before the existing flow converted well.
  • Custom UI per partner: one standard flow instead of bespoke builds.
  • A big-bang launch: we rolled out in phases so problems showed up early and at small scale.

Experiments and execution

  • Integration fixes with Silpo's team for timeouts and API errors.
  • Error handling and retry: clear messages for each failure reason and a one-tap retry, so a recoverable failure didn't turn into a lost order.
  • Phased rollout to Silpo e-commerce customers, then loyalty integration with the CPO.

Results

MetricBeforeAfter
Successful payment conversion88%93% (+5 points)
Users served—10,000+

What I learned

Measure every failure code. A single success rate hides the problem. Breaking failures down by reason showed which ones were fixable, and that's where the +5 points came from.
