TrackaAI: a Linear-style tracker with AI teammates

  • LinksSide project · GitHub
  • RoleFounder-PM and builder (solo, AI-assisted)
  • TimelineSep 2026 – now
  • LocationRemote

TL;DR

Problem
Small teams lose time writing tickets, splitting work and triaging boards. Existing trackers bolt AI on as a text box, not as a teammate that does real work.
My role
Wrote the PRD (goals, non-goals, domain model, plans, success metrics), then built and shipped it with Claude.
Team
Solo, with AI.
Outcome
Working product: teams, workspaces, realtime Kanban, five AI features (write, break down, ask, board copilot and AI teammates) and Free/Lite/Pro plans with metered AI runs. [Add: deployment status and any usage]

Context and why it mattered

Linear set the bar for fast, keyboard-first issue tracking. The opportunity was to make AI a real participant on the board, not a text box. The goal was for it to write issues, split them, answer questions about the board, and take assigned work through to review. [Add: why you built this, e.g. your own pain as a PM]

The PRD set measurable onboarding goals from day one: a team can sign up, create a team, invite teammates and be working on a board in under 3 minutes.

What I said no to (v1 non-goals): native mobile apps and offline mode; cycles, roadmaps, Gantt charts and time tracking; Git integrations, a public API and webhooks; SSO/SAML and audit logs. I also chose simulated billing first, with Stripe to replace it later, so pricing could be tested without payment setup.

Experiments and execution

  • Onboarding wizard: create team → first workspace → invite (skippable) → land on a seeded default board.
  • AI with guardrails: every change the copilot wants to make on the board shows up as a card you approve first. AI acts with the permissions of the person using it, and the AI teammate's work always goes to review.
  • Cost-aware models: a cheap model (Haiku) for writing and breakdowns, and a stronger one (Sonnet) for the copilot and agents. Each request counts as one "AI run" and is metered per plan.
  • Packaging: Free (1 person, 10 runs a month), Lite ($10: 3 people, 100 runs) and Pro ($25: unlimited, copilot and AI teammates).
  • Built to swap backends: a mock data layer shipped first, so the UI could be built before the database. Supabase was then swapped in without UI changes.
