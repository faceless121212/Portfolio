Web-to-app funnels and paywall experiments

  • Company7motiff
  • RoleGrowth Product Manager (part-time, remote)
  • TimelineSep 2026 – now

TL;DR

Problem
Turn paid ad clicks into installs and paying subscribers for an EdTech app, across web and app.
My role
Growth PM owning the funnel from first ad click to install and purchase, plus paywall, pricing and onboarding experiments.
Team
Me, performance marketing and developers.
Outcome
This engagement is under NDA, so results aren't public. This page shows the approach and process instead.

Under NDA. The product and company details and all performance numbers are confidential. This page describes my approach and process only.

Context and why it mattered

In a web-to-app funnel, a web quiz qualifies and motivates the user before install. Each step either compounds conversion or leaks it: the ad, the quiz, the result, the paywall, the install and the first session.

Discovery and data

I map the funnel from first ad click to purchase and look for the largest leaks: quiz completion, result to paywall, paywall to trial start, trial to paid, and install to first lesson.

Hypotheses and prioritization

Example hypothesis: a personalised quiz result screen, one that reflects the user's own answers back to them, increases paywall-to-trial conversion compared with a generic result.

What I said no to: too many parallel tests. I limit how many experiments run at once so their results stay clean and can be attributed.

Experiments and execution

  • Quiz funnel iterations: question order, length and personalisation.
  • Paywall and pricing experiments for trial starts and subscription conversion.
  • Onboarding iterations to drive early activation in the app.

Results

Withheld under NDA. I'm happy to walk through the method in an interview.

What I learned

The paywall is a product. Paywall design and pricing move revenue as much as features do, so they deserve the same discovery and experiment discipline.
