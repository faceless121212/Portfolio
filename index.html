How I work

Four principles

  1. Find the drop-off first

    Before ideas, the funnel. I map every step, find where people leave and size the gap, so the team works on the leak that matters.

  2. Experiment, don't guess

    Every change starts as a hypothesis with a metric and a decision rule. A/B tests settle debates faster than meetings do.

  3. Small steps that compound

    +1–3% at each onboarding step adds up to +5% registrations. I ship narrow fixes often instead of waiting for a big redesign.

  4. Ship with engineering and design, not to them

    I bring the problem and the data, and we shape the solution together. Shared context makes for better calls and faster delivery.
